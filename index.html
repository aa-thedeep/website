The Deep

The prototyping shop and makerspace of MIT AeroAstro, where research, classes, and design-build teams turn designs into hardware.

Get started See the calendar

Parts by Varun Maniar

A departmental shop built on makerspace culture

The Deep is a departmental shop inspired by makerspace culture and peer mentoring. We support the educational and research missions of AeroAstro by facilitating prototyping and the construction of experimental apparatus, advanced vehicles, and materials.

The shop frequently turns out parts for liquid- and solid-fueled rockets, spacecraft (especially CubeSats), and autonomous submersibles, along with research apparatus for biotech, microfluidics, robotics, and mechatronics. People here micromachine titanium and exotic engineering ceramics.

How we run it

  • Orientations, operating hours, and scheduled trainings are posted on a public calendar.
  • Trainings and open hours are run by both staff and volunteer mentors.
  • A catalog of beginner-to-advanced co-curricular trainings builds competency and safety.
